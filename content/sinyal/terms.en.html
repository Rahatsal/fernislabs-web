1. What Sinyal is

Sinyal helps you look at visible facial and body-language cues more carefully. You can:

  • analyse the body language in one photo;
  • analyse the “compatibility” between two people from two photos or a video of up to 10 seconds;
  • learn cues with the in-app dictionary;
  • play the Signal Hunt game, finding cues in ready-made photos, and compete in its seasonal ranking; and
  • with Pro, use the AI coach and the other Pro features described in section 8.

Analyses are produced by an AI model (Google’s Gemini API) and returned as a structured text report. Before any media is sent for analysis the app asks for your explicit consent.

2. What an analysis can and cannot do

  • An analysis is an interpretation of visible signals only, such as posture, facial expression or distance, in a single moment or a short clip.
  • It cannot determine with certainty anyone’s intent, emotions, whether they are lying or being deceptive, their personality, their health or how a relationship will turn out.
  • AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Context the camera does not see — culture, health, the situation, what happened before — can change the meaning completely.
  • Sinyal is not professional advice of any kind: not medical, psychological or therapeutic, legal, or human-resources advice. If you need such help, talk to a qualified professional.
  • A “compatibility” result describes how the visible cues between two people might be read. It is not a prediction about your relationship.

You are responsible for how you use a report and for any decision you take. Do not treat a report as evidence about a person.

7. Signal Hunt and the ranking

Signal Hunt (Sinyal Avı) is a free, optional game in which you try to find body-language cues in ready-made photos. Right answers earn XP. Each calendar month (in UTC) is a new season, and the XP you earn during a season sets your place in a ranking that other players can see. What the ranking shows is explained in the “Signal Hunt and the ranking” section of the Privacy Policy.

Fair play

  • Play yourself. Do not use bots, scripts or other automated tools, or a modified app, and do not try to find answers by automated means.
  • Do not exploit bugs or loopholes. If you find one, please tell us.
  • Do not create more than one account, play on someone else’s account or let someone else play on yours to climb the ranking.

Hunter names

  • You choose the hunter name shown in the ranking, and you are responsible for it.
  • It must not contain insults, swear words, hate speech, sexual content or anything that infringes other people’s rights.
  • It must not impersonate, or suggest a connection with, another person, a brand or Sinyal.
  • It must not contain personal or contact details, such as an email address, phone number or social media handle.
  • We check names against an automatic word list, but this check does not catch everything, and an unsuitable name remains your responsibility.

If the rules are broken

If we find that these rules have been broken, or a name is reported to us as unsuitable, we may change or hide the hunter name, reset or correct XP and season scores, remove the player from the ranking or restrict their access to the game. For serious or repeated breaches, section 14 also applies. If you see an unsuitable name, please email us.

XP has no monetary value

  • XP, streaks, places, medals and season results are in-game indicators only. They have no monetary value and cannot be sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, Pro or any other reward. Signal Hunt does not award cash prizes or other prizes.
  • With Pro, lives (owls) refill faster, so Pro subscribers can play more often. The XP an answer earns is calculated by the same rules for everyone.

Changes

We may change the game’s rules, scoring, season length, how the ranking is shown and the questions; correct season results because of a bug or cheating; or withdraw the game entirely (see section 11).
