Sinyal

Your photo’s subtitles.

Sinyal uses AI to interpret visible body-language cues in a photo or a short video, and tells it with a smile.

Coming soon toApp StoreComing soon toGoogle Play

What it does

Your body’s subtitles
Your shoulders, hands and gaze each say a line. Turn a card over to see the visible cue and what it may mean.
Your bodies’ chat
Photos of two people, or a 10-second video: where you meet, where you differ, and a compatibility reading.
Dictionary and Sinyal Avı
Learn body language in short lessons, then hunt for cues in real photos and climb the season ranking.
AI coach
With Pro, ask a coach questions about your report.

Free and Pro

Free
3 analyses a day, the first lines of every report and one free full report.
Pro
Every line and its why, the real translation, the AI coach, a higher daily limit and reopening past reports. Weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription.

Privacy at a glance

  • We do not store your photos or videos; they are used only for the analysis.
  • The app asks for your consent before every analysis.
  • No ads, no tracking, your data is not sold.
  • Delete your account in the app or with the steps on this site.

Read the full Privacy Policy

Sinyal only offers interpretations of visible cues. It does not detect lies, emotions or intent with certainty, and it is not medical or psychological advice. For ages 18 and over.
